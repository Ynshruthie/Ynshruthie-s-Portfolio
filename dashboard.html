Frequently Asked Questions

What type of collaborations do you accept?

I accept sponsored Instagram posts & reels, YouTube dedicated/integrated videos, UGC ad creation, long-term ambassadorships, and select product gifting campaigns that align with my audience.

What is your typical turnaround time?

Standard delivery time is 7–10 business days after receiving the product. Rush delivery (3–5 business days) is also available upon request.

Do you create UGC content for ads?

Yes! I create direct-to-brand UGC content in raw 4K with multiple hooks, call-to-actions, and ad usage rights suitable for Meta and TikTok ad campaigns.

Can we discuss a long-term partnership?

Absolutely! I love building sustained relationships with brands. Multi-month packages and retainer partnerships include discounted rates and prioritized scheduling.

Do you accept gifting collaborations?

I accept gifting if the product is an organic fit for my lifestyle and skincare routine. For guaranteed posts and dedicated reviews, a commercial fee applies.

How do we get started?

Simply fill out the form above or email me. I will review your requirements, confirm availability, and send across a customized proposal & contract!
